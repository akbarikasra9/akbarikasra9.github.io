# Synergy Report

Our team consisted of Kasra Akbari, Soha Arian, and Devin Nemetz-Simmons. We divided the work across the four required visualizations while also reviewing the project together to make sure the final submission was consistent.

Kasra worked primarily on Charts 1 and 2. Chart 1 was the variable-width column chart comparing population and mean HPI across continents, while Chart 2 compared multiple wellbeing indicators for the 20 most populous countries. Kasra also organized the Quarto page and completed the final editing so that the charts followed a consistent structure and presentation style.

Soha worked primarily on Chart 3, which compared the top 20 and bottom 20 countries by HPI in 2025. Soha also helped check the data and results to make sure the values used in the visualizations were consistent with the Happy Planet Index dataset.

Devin worked primarily on Chart 4, which compared mean life satisfaction across continents. Devin also helped review the final project and worked on the GitHub side of the submission.

After the individual pieces were completed, we brought the charts together in the same Quarto project and reviewed the overall presentation. We focused on keeping the charts readable, using clear titles and labels, including source captions, and maintaining a reasonably consistent visual style across the assignment.

If we completed the project again, we would establish the shared visual theme and file structure before beginning the individual charts. This would reduce the amount of formatting and adjustment needed when combining everyone's work at the end. We would also test the complete Quarto document from a clean R session earlier in the process so that package, file-path, or reproducibility issues could be identified before the final review.
