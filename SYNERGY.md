## How We Combined the Parts

We divided the project by chart type while working with the same World Bank dataset. Each chart addressed a different aspect of our research question: whether countries that spend more on healthcare tend to have longer life expectancies.

After developing the individual charts, we integrated them into one R Shiny dashboard. We used shared reactive filters so users could select continents and healthcare spending ranges and compare the same countries across all three charts.

We also worked toward consistent chart labels, colors, and explanations to make the dashboard easier to navigate and understand. The completed application was deployed through Posit Connect Cloud and embedded on our project website.

## What Teamwork Made Better

Our collaboration helped us examine the research question from three different perspectives instead of relying on a single visualization.

The scatterplot shows the relationship between healthcare spending and life expectancy. The bubble chart adds GDP per capita to explore economic differences, while the histogram reveals how healthcare spending is distributed across countries.

Combining these charts made the dashboard more informative because users can examine relationships, economic context, and distributions within one application. Dividing responsibilities also allowed us to focus on individual visualizations while contributing to a shared final product.

## What We Would Do Differently

For future projects, we would establish a shared design and coding structure earlier so that integrating the charts requires fewer adjustments.

We would also test the reactive filters and dashboard layout throughout development rather than primarily during final integration. This would give us more time to improve usability, troubleshoot technical issues, and collect feedback before deployment.
